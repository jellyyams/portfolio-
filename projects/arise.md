Working as a junior expert at Demos Helsinki, I played a key role in supporting the Social Science and Humanities (SSH) workpackage of the ARISE project. ARISE (Agile, human-centric, and Real-tIme enabled open SourcE technologies advancing industrial HRI in Europe) is a Horizon Europe-funded project that aims to make industrial human-robot interaction (HRI) more accessible, affordable, and easy to adopt. The particular focus of the project is on HRI in healthcare, intralogistics and manufacturing. The project began in January 2024 and is planned to conclude in June 2027.

Prior to my start at Demos Helsinki, the ARISE team had formulated an SSH framework that outlined 8 principles for developing human-centric robotics, such as Worker Dignity and Autonomy, Accountability, and Respect for Human Agency. The SSH framework also includes an overview of legislation, ethical guidelines, standards, and design practices that provide guidance on how to protect fundamental rights and align with societal goals during the technology development process.

During the time I worked on the project, I helped develop presentations, curriculum, and support material for mentoring technology developers on how to integrate the ARISE SSH framework into their workflow. I took part in key strategic conversations on how to translate the social and ethical considerations of the framework into actionable criteria for technology developers. I also built a React website that presented the ARISE SSH framework as an interactive and accessible digital experience. Our aim was not only to provide the tools for technologists to reflect on the social and ethical implications of their work, but also understand how we could iterate on the SSH framework to better align with the norms and framings of the technology development industry.
