_Growth_ visualizes the history of Microsoft through its 276 acquisitions of other tech companies between its founding in 1975 and 2023. Viewers are able to scroll through the history of Microsoft and watch as companies emerge and co-exist, before eventually being drawn into Microsoft’s membrane of acquired companies. The aesthetics of the visualization are inspired by microbial processes, prompting viewers to examine Microsoft as a living microorganism that grows, contracts, and interacts with other organisms in its environment in consumptive ways.

Note that not all acquisition data is publicly accessible, and the data that is available may not always be verifiable. The visualization is based on data that is avaialble on the [Microsoft Acquisition wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_mergers_and_acquisitions_by_Microsoft) and [Microsoft's Investor Relations webpage](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/acquisition-history). 

The project was written in JavaScript and built with the D3.js library. Node movements are driven by the library's force simulator. 

This project was featured at the [2025 Visualizing Knowledge conference](https://vizknowledge.aalto.fi) at Aalto University as part of the student showcase. You can interact with the visualization at [this page](https://jellyyams.github.io/growth/).
